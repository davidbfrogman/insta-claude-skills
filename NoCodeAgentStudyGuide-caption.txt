The hard part of building an AI agent was never the code. It's deciding what the thing is allowed to do.

That's why non-coders can do this now. The building got easy. The deciding didn't, and the deciding is the part you're already good at.

So I pulled ten free courses and put them in the order that actually works.

STAGE 1 · Understand the system before you touch a tool
1. Anthropic, "Building Effective Agents." Clearest mental model out there, and its main argument is a subtraction: most of what you want doesn't need an agent.
2. Botpress Academy, 35 minutes. Agent projects rarely fail in the building. They fail because nobody could say in one sentence what the thing was for.

STAGE 2 · Build your first one
3. Zapier AI Builder Path, 2 hrs. The gentlest on-ramp. You need one thing running on real data this weekend.
4. Make Academy Foundation. Zapier teaches you to think in lines. Make teaches you to think in shapes.

STAGE 3 · Design the conversation
5. Voiceflow Basics. Every agent that talks to a human needs a plan for the moment it doesn't understand. Most first drafts have a happy path and a cliff.

STAGE 4 · Connect it to real data
6. n8n Quickstart. The stretch course. Also where you learn why agents make things up: nothing told them where to look.
7. Relevance AI. Two agents aren't twice as good as one. They're one agent plus a handoff, and the handoff is where quality leaks out.

STAGE 5 · See how enterprises do it
8. Salesforce Agentblazer. Whose data, which records, what can it change, who's accountable when it's wrong.
9. Microsoft Copilot Studio. Do the one your company already pays for. Skim the other.

STAGE 6 · Open the hood
10. Flowise docs. Everything the managed platforms politely hid from you, on one screen.

All ten are free. All verified this month.
