THE AI ENABLEMENT PIVOT
How to move a career you already have into the job companies cannot staff.

---

Somewhere in your company is a paid AI seat nobody has opened in six weeks. The license was approved, the demo went well, and then usage did what usage does. It spiked, it drifted, and now a handful of enthusiasts carry the entire adoption number.

That gap, between what a company bought and what its people actually do on a Tuesday afternoon, has become a job. Most often it is called AI enablement.

Read a stack of those postings and you notice how little of the work is about AI. It is use case discovery, workflow redesign, role based training, internal communications, guardrails, champion networks, and measurement. That is adoption work, pointed at a newer and stranger tool. If you have run an onboarding program, launched a platform nobody asked for, built a curriculum, or dragged a cross functional project across the line, you have already done the harder half.

What you are missing is evidence that you have done it with AI in the middle. This guide is about closing that one specific gap, and it is a month of deliberate work rather than a career restart.

The premise, stated plainly: you are not applying as an AI person. You are applying as an adoption person who has done the AI part firsthand, and companies are quietly discovering they need the second one more than they thought.

Not a prompting course and not a technical track. There is no code in it, and it will not teach you to build models or evaluate them. It also will not tell you the market is easy. It will tell you which thirty days of work make you credible in it.
