THE NON-CODER'S STUDY GUIDE TO BUILDING AI AGENTS
Ten free courses, in the order that actually works.

---

Most material on building AI agents is written by engineers, for engineers. It opens with a framework, a repository, and an architecture diagram, and gets around to explaining what the thing is supposed to accomplish somewhere near the end.

If you are an operator, a marketer, a consultant, a founder, or a product manager, that order is backwards. You already know the work. What you are missing is the shape of the machine.

This guide runs the other way. It starts with how to think about the problem, moves to tools you can use by dragging boxes around a screen, and only then goes anywhere near a technical detail. Every resource in it is free to study. Every stage ends with something you build.

The premise, stated plainly: the building got easy, the deciding did not, and the deciding is the part you are already good at.

· The six components every working agent has, and how to spot which one is missing when yours misbehaves

· The capstone: one small agent, six components, and the ten test cases that separate people who built an agent from people who can build agents
· A stuck-point lookup table. Symptom in, next step out.
· An honest section on the two places this gets uncomfortable, so neither one reads as evidence you are the wrong person for it

WHO IT'S FOR

Operators, marketers, consultants, founders, product managers, and anyone else who wants to understand agentic systems without first becoming a software developer.

WHAT IT ISN'T

Not a framework tutorial. No code, no repository, no API keys needed to follow the argument. It will not make you an engineer and it is not trying to.
